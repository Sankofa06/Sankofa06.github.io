Why this repository exists

ComfyChat is the focused mobile cockpit in the AI-workspace family: narrower than LM Manager Pro, more product-shaped than the LM Laboratory workbench, and further along the native fleet path than the clean-slate Mira experiment.

LOCAL-FIRST BY DESIGN

Your models. Your machines. Your files.

ComfyChat is not a hosted AI service. It is a mobile command center for the local stack you already run, with on-device capability where supported and explicit routes to configured engines.
